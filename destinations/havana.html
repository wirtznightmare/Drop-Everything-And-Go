// Caribbean · Cuba

Havana & Trinidad

The world's most perfectly preserved time capsule — crumbling, musical and unforgettable.

💰 £40–£70/day 🗓️ Best: Nov–Apr 🛂 Tourist card required (~£25) 💬 Spanish

1958, still running

Cuba is the only country in the world that looks like 1958. The same Chevrolets and Buicks, the same colonial architecture, the same social system largely in place. The travel experience is unlike anything else.

Havana's Malecón at sunset, the jazz clubs of Old Havana, son cubano playing in dim bars off Obispo — these experiences exist nowhere else on earth. Trinidad, three hours east, is a UNESCO-preserved colonial city barely changed since the 18th century.

// Quick Facts

💵
Cash Only
US cards do not work. UK/EU cards unreliable. Bring full trip budget in EUR or GBP cash.
📶
WiFi
State-controlled, available through ETECSA cards only. Very limited connectivity.
🏡
Casas
Casas particulares (private licensed rooms) are far better than state hotels.
🎫
Tourist Card
Required — buy from airline or Cuban embassy. About £25.
🎵
Music
The music bars of Old Havana play live son, salsa and bolero every night.
// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Havana Malecón at Sunset
The 8km seawall at dusk — fishermen, couples, musicians and the crumbling city behind. One of the great city promenades anywhere in the world.
02
Classic Car Tour of Havana
A 1950s American convertible through Old Havana, Revolution Square and Vedado — the only way to travel in a country with more pristine 1950s cars than anywhere on earth.
03
Trinidad — the Preserved Colonial City
UNESCO-protected cobblestone plazas, colonial churches and the Valley of the Sugar Mills — the economy that funded colonial Cuba, still visible from the hilltop.
04
Son Cubano & Salsa in Old Havana
The music bars play live every night — the local Casa de la Música is more authentic than the tourist bars on Obispo.
05
Viñales Valley — Tobacco Country
Limestone mogotes rising from tobacco plantations — cycle between farms and watch hand-rolled cigars being made.
06
El Morro Castle at Night
The 16th-century fortress at the harbour entrance — the cannon ceremony at 9pm and the view back over the illuminated Malecón.
// Areas & Neighbourhoods

Where to base yourself

Old Havana (Habana Vieja)
Colonial architecture, music
The UNESCO World Heritage quarter — most beautiful part of the city. Stay in a casa particular here.
Vedado
Local life, Art Deco
The residential Art Deco quarter — more authentic restaurants and the extraordinary Necrópolis Cristóbal Colón cemetery.
Trinidad
Colonial preservation, beach nearby
The perfectly preserved 18th-century colonial city — cobblestones, salsa schools and La Boca beach nearby.

// When to Go

Season guide

Nov–Apr: Dry season — best time. Dec–Jan: Peak season. Jun–Oct: Hurricane season — heavy rain, prices lower.

// Need to Know

Before you go

💵

Bring full budget in Euros or GBP cash — US cards do not work at all.

📶

Connectivity is very limited. ETECSA Wi-Fi cards available at Etecsa offices.

🏡

Casas particulares are the accommodation of choice — book via Airbnb or direct.

🎫

Tourist card is different from a visa — check requirements for your nationality.

🌡️

Cuba is hot and humid year-round — lightweight clothes and strong mosquito repellent essential.
