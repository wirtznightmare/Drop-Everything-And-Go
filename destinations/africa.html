// Africa

Africa
The continent that changes everyone who visits

From the Masai Mara at dawn to the Sahara at dusk — Africa is the most emotionally powerful continent on earth for travel.

Africa defies the single narrative most people arrive with. It is not one place — it is 54 countries with wildly different cultures, landscapes, infrastructures and travel experiences. The East African safari circuit (Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda) is exceptional and increasingly accessible. North Africa (Morocco, Egypt) is ancient and extraordinary. Southern Africa (South Africa, Zambia, Zimbabwe) is spectacular and infrastructure-rich. Start with what draws you most. Safari? East Africa, July–October for the Great Migration. Beaches? Zanzibar, June–October. History? Egypt and Morocco year-round. Culture? South Africa has the best urban culture on the continent.

// When to Go

Season guide

East Africa (Kenya, Tanzania): Dry seasons June–October and January–February are best for wildlife viewing. July–October for the Great Migration river crossings specifically. North Africa (Morocco, Egypt): October–April is ideal — summers are extremely hot. Ramadan changes the atmosphere significantly — check dates before booking. Southern Africa: May–October is the dry season — best for wildlife and general travel. West Africa: November–March is the dry season across the Sahel belt.

// Need to Know

Before you go

ℹ️

Vaccinations required or recommended for most of Africa: Yellow Fever (proof required for some countries), Typhoid, Hepatitis A, Malaria prophylaxis for sub-Saharan Africa.

ℹ️

Safari is not as expensive as the reputation suggests — Kenya and Tanzania both have budget camping options. The luxury lodges are extraordinary but not the only option.

ℹ️

E-Visa systems now cover most of East and Southern Africa — Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, South Africa and Ethiopia all have online visa applications.

ℹ️

Always carry US dollars in small denominations as backup — widely accepted across East and Southern Africa where card payment can be unreliable.

ℹ️

The Indian Ocean island combo (Zanzibar + Mombasa, or Madagascar) is one of the world's great beach destinations and pairs perfectly with a Kenya safari.
