// South America · Brazil

Rio de Janeiro

The world's most spectacular urban setting — mountains, Atlantic and 7 million people.

💰 £35–£70/day 🗓️ Best: May–Sep 🛂 Check current e-Visa requirements 💬 Portuguese

Mountain, sea and pure energy

Rio de Janeiro is the world's most beautifully situated city. Sugar Loaf and Corcovado bracket the city, Copacabana and Ipanema face the Atlantic, and the favela communities climb the hills in between. Nothing prepares you for the combination.

The city rewards those who engage with its complexity — the responsible favela tours, the Lapa samba clubs, the local boteco bars in Botafogo — rather than staying on the beachfront.

// Quick Facts

💰
Daily Budget
£35–70. Ipanema is expensive. Street food and local bars are cheap.
🎭
Carnival
February — book accommodation 12 months ahead, prices multiply 5–10×.
🛡️

Use Uber. Avoid displaying phones. Favela tours with local accredited operators only.
🛂
Visa
Check current Brazilian e-Visa requirements at gov.br/pf before booking.
🇧🇷
Language
Portuguese, not Spanish — the distinction matters to Brazilians.
// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Sugarloaf at Sunset
Two cable car stages to the granite monolith — the view of the bay, Christ the Redeemer on the opposite hill and the city below at golden hour.
02
Christ the Redeemer at Dawn
The first cog train for the statue at sunrise above the clouds — when it clears, the panorama is extraordinary.
03
Copacabana & Ipanema Beach Culture
The social ritual of the city beach — football, vendors, the 7am morning dip before the heat. Best before 11am.
04
Responsible Favela Tour — Vidigal
Community-based guided tour with local operators from the favela itself — the most honest cultural experience available in Rio.
05
Lapa — Selarón Steps & Samba
The mosaic staircase and the samba clubs that run past 6am on weekends.
06
Botanical Garden & Santa Teresa
The 1808 botanical garden and the creative hilltop neighbourhood with the historic tram.

// When to Go

Season guide

May–Sep: Dry season — best for Rio. Clear skies, comfortable temperatures. Feb–Mar: Carnival — extraordinary but book 12 months ahead. Oct–Jan: Hot and rainy season.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🛡️

Use Uber not street taxis. Keep phones in bags. Be aware after dark in Lapa and Centro.

🇧🇷

Portuguese, not Spanish — Brazilians appreciate the distinction.

🎭

Carnival accommodation must be booked 12 months ahead.

🌊

Rip currents on Rio's beaches are serious — swim between the flags.

🚡

The Santa Teresa tram is the most charming transport in Rio.
