Fiji
Fiji

The friendliest people and the clearest shallow-water reefs in the Pacific.

Oceania · Pacific Islands

Why visit Fiji?

Fiji has a reputation for expensive resorts. But the guesthouse island circuit — the Yasawa and Mamanuca chains accessed by the Yasawa Flyer catamaran — offers the same coral reefs, the same turquoise water and the same extraordinary Fijian hospitality for a fraction of the overwater villa price.

The coral reefs are genuinely world-class in their accessibility and visibility. The Fijian greeting (bula!) is one of the warmest in the world and entirely genuine.

Who It's Perfect For
🤿

Divers & Snorkellers

The Great Astrolabe Reef, the Somosomo Strait and the Beqa Lagoon shark dive are among the Pacific's finest dive sites.

🏝️

Island Hoppers

The Yasawa and Mamanuca island chains offer the classic Pacific island experience — guesthouses, reef snorkelling and Fijian village life at affordable prices.

💑

Honeymooners & Couples

Fiji's luxury resorts — Laucala, Turtle Island, Tokoriki — are some of the world's finest, and the natural setting makes everything more beautiful.
