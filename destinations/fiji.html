// Oceania · Pacific Islands

Fiji

The friendliest people and the clearest shallow-water reefs in the Pacific.

💰 £40–£80/day 🗓️ Best: May–Oct 🛂 No visa on arrival (most nationalities, 4 months) 💬 English, Fijian, Hindi

Coral reefs, genuine warmth

Fiji has a reputation for expensive resorts, and some are genuinely extraordinary. But the guesthouse island circuit — the Yasawa and Mamanuca chains accessed by the Yasawa Flyer catamaran — offers the same reefs, the same turquoise water and the same extraordinary Fijian hospitality for a fraction of the overwater villa price.

The coral reefs are genuinely world-class in their accessibility and visibility. The Fijian greeting (bula!) is one of the warmest in the world and entirely genuine. Fiji is one of the Pacific's most complete and most accessible island destinations.

// Quick Facts

💰
Daily Budget
£40–80. Guesthouse islands much cheaper than resorts — same reefs.
🚢
Yasawa Flyer
The catamaran connecting the island chain — buy a Bula Pass for multi-island hopping.
🤿
Reef Diving
Some of the world's best soft coral diving — the Great Astrolabe Reef and the Beqa Lagoon shark dives.
☀️
Climate
Tropical — hot and humid year-round. May–Oct (dry season) best for reefs.
🏝️
Guesthouses
Bula Backpackers, Mantaray Island, Oarsman's Bay Lodge — the guesthouse circuit is the real Fiji.
// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Yasawa Islands Guesthouse Circuit
The island hopping circuit by Yasawa Flyer catamaran — Nacula, Naviti and Tavewa islands have the best guesthouses and some of the clearest shallow reefs in the Pacific.
02
Blue Lagoon Snorkelling — Nanuya Island
The famous blue lagoon filmed in the 1980 movie — turquoise water, white sand and incredible reef snorkelling accessible directly from the beach.
03
Beqa Lagoon Shark Dive
The world's most famous shark dive — 8 species including bull sharks hand-fed by experienced divemasters in 30m visibility water.
04
Navala Village Homestay
The only surviving thatched bure village in Fiji — an overnight homestay with traditional welcome ceremony (sevusevu), kava and sleeping in a traditional bure.
05
Mamanuca Islands — Castaway Island
The island used in the Tom Hanks film — now a resort, but the surrounding reef and the Mamanuca chain generally are among the best day trips from Nadi.
06
Taveuni — Bouma National Heritage Park
The Garden Island with the 156m Tavoro Waterfalls and the International Date Line running through it.
// Areas & Neighbourhoods

Where to base yourself

Yasawa Islands
Best guesthouses, snorkelling
The 20-island chain accessible by Yasawa Flyer — the most affordable and authentic Fiji experience.
Mamanuca Islands

The main island with the airport — Nadi is the transit hub, Suva is the capital with the best Fijian cultural museums.

// When to Go

Season guide

May–Oct: Dry season — the best time. Clear skies, low humidity and the best reef visibility. Nov–Apr: Wet season — heavy rain and possible cyclones December–March. Significantly cheaper.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🚢

Buy the Bula Pass for the Yasawa Flyer before arriving — various durations available at the Port Denarau ferry terminal in Nadi.

🤿

Bring your own reef-safe sunscreen and a rashguard — Fiji's reefs are extraordinary and worth protecting.

🏝️

Guesthouse islands (Nacula, Naviti, Tavewa) offer the same reefs as £500/night resorts for £40–60/night including meals.

🪴

Bring a gift of kava root (waka) when visiting any Fijian village — it's the traditional protocol.

✈️

Nadi International Airport is the entry point — most international flights. Fiji Airways flies from Sydney, Auckland, Los Angeles and London.
