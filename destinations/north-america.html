// North America

North
America

New York, Los Angeles, Vancouver, Banff and world-class powder from BC to Utah.

North America rewards those who go beyond the obvious. New York's mythology matches the reality — the Brooklyn Bridge at dawn, the Louvre-scale Met Museum, the borough food scenes that no travel guide fully captures. Los Angeles defies its reputation once you leave Hollywood. Canada contains some of the most extraordinary wilderness on earth within 2 hours of a major international airport.

// When to Go

Season guide

New York: Apr–Jun and Sep–Nov are ideal — summer is hot and crowded. Los Angeles: Mar–May and Sep–Nov for the best weather. Canada (Summer): Jun–Sep for Banff and Vancouver — the Icefields Parkway at its most accessible. Canada (Winter): Dec–Mar for Whistler and Northern Lights in the Yukon.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🛂

ESTA required for UK/EU nationals visiting the US ($21). Canada eTA (CA$7) required for most.

🚗

A car is essential in Los Angeles and for the Canadian Rockies — distances are too large for public transport.

💵

Tipping in the US: 18–22% at restaurants, 15–20% for taxis. Not optional.

🦺

Canadian Working Holiday Visa for under-35 Western nationals — turns a trip into a year-long adventure.

🌡️

New York and Chicago winters are extreme — pack accordingly if visiting Dec–Feb.
