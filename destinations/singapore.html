// Southeast Asia

Singapore

The world's most efficient city-state — extraordinary food, Marina Bay and a garden city.

💰 £60–£100/day 🗓️ Best: Year-round (Feb–Apr driest) 🛂 No visa (most nationalities, 30–90 days) 💬 English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil

Four cultures, one extraordinary city

Singapore is the most efficiently run city on earth and the most diverse — Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam (Arab Quarter) and the Colonial District all within walking distance of each other, each completely distinct.

The Hawker Centres are the real attraction — the multi-ethnic street food culture that Singapore has elevated to a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage. A Singapore Hawker meal is one of the great food experiences in Asia for £2–4 per dish.

// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Hawker Centre Crawl — Maxwell & Chinatown Complex
The UNESCO street food culture — Tian Tian chicken rice (Maxwell), Hokkien mee, char kway teow and chilli crab from the best hawker stalls in Singapore.
02
Gardens by the Bay — Supertrees at Night
The 18 vertical garden towers illuminate at night in a free light show. The conservatories (extra ticket) contain extraordinary tropical and Mediterranean plantings.
03
Marina Bay Sands Rooftop — Infinity Pool View
The world's most photographed rooftop pool (hotel guests only) but the free SkyPark Observation Deck gives the same view.
04
Chinatown, Little India & Kampong Glam
Three completely different ethnic neighbourhoods within walking distance — temples, mosques, incense and the most diverse street food in Asia.
05
Sentosa Island — Universal Studios
The resort island with Universal Studios, the cable car and Palawan Beach — the most family-friendly day in Singapore.
06
Night Safari — the World's First
The world's first nocturnal zoo — 120 animal species observed in near-natural habitat at night.

// When to Go

Season guide

Feb–Apr: The driest and least humid months — best for outdoor exploration. Nov–Jan: Northeast monsoon — heavy rain. Singapore is fundamentally year-round; rain comes in intense short showers rather than all-day drizzle.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🚇

The MRT is the best and cheapest way to navigate — tap-and-go with any contactless card.

🍜

A hawker meal (3 dishes + drink) for £6–8 is one of the world's great food experiences. Maxwell Road and Chinatown Complex are the benchmarks.

🌿

Gardens by the Bay Supertree Grove is free every night from 7:45pm for the light show — no ticket needed.

⚖️

Singapore's laws are genuinely strict — no chewing gum sold, drug offences carry severe penalties. Treat the rules seriously.

🔌

Singapore is an excellent transit point — a 1–2 night stopover on the way to Australia, Bali or elsewhere is well worth it.
