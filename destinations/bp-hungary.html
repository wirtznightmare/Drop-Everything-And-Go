Backpacking Hungary

Hungary punches significantly above its weight for a budget destination. Budapest has 119 thermal bath establishments fed by natural hot springs, a ruin bar scene that has transformed a derelict neighbourhood into one of Europe's great nightlife destinations, and a Parliament building that is the most dramatic on the continent. Excellent value throughout.

The Highlights

8 Things to Do in Hungary

01
Visit Széchenyi Baths on a weekday morning — the famous outdoor pools in Neo-Baroque splendour
02
Walk up Castle Hill for Fisherman's Bastion — the best Parliament and Danube view in Budapest
03
Explore the Ruin Bar district (Szimpla Kert and beyond) on a Thursday–Saturday night
04
Book the Parliament Interior tour — the Holy Crown of Hungary is extraordinary
05
Great Market Hall for paprika, lángos and traditional Hungarian embroidery
06
Dohány Street Synagogue — the largest in Europe and a profound Jewish Quarter
07
Jewish Quarter walking tour — the most intact Jewish neighbourhood in Central Europe
08
Sunset Danube cruise — Parliament and Chain Bridge illuminated from the water

Insider Knowledge

What Most Guides Don't Tell You About Hungary

  • →

    Hungary uses Forint (HUF) — not the Euro. Exchange at banks or ATMs, never at exchange bureaus

  • →

    Hungarian food is far more than goulash — try lángos, halászlé (fish soup), Mangalica pork and kürtőskalács

  • →

    The ruin bar scene peaks Thursday–Saturday. Sunday morning at Szimpla is a farmers' market — different atmosphere, equally good

  • →

    Pre-book the Parliament interior tour online — it fills up weeks ahead and is the most popular attraction

  • →

    The Budapest Card covers all transport and entry to some museums — check if it suits your itinerary
