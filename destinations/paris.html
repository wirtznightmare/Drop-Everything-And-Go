// Western Europe · France

Paris

The most visited city on earth. Still extraordinary when you know where to look.

💰 £70–£120/day 🗓️ Best: Apr–Jun & Sep–Oct 🛂 No visa (Schengen, 90 days) 💬 French (English in most tourist businesses)

The city that invented culture

Paris is the most visited city on earth for reasons that remain completely valid. The Louvre is the greatest art museum in human history. The Eiffel Tower at night from the Trocadéro is genuinely one of the most beautiful things you will see. The 7th arrondissement at 8am with fresh croissants is not a cliché — it is a perfect morning.

The key to Paris is escaping the obvious. Montmartre at dusk, the covered passages of the 2nd arrondissement, the Canal Saint-Martin on a Sunday, the wine bars of the 11th at midnight — these are the parts of Paris that feel like they belong to you.

// Quick Facts

💰
Daily Budget
£70–120. Budget boulangerie meals £4–8. Museum Pass (2-day, £50) covers Louvre and Orsay.
🗓️
Best Time
Apr–Jun for spring and long evenings. Sep–Oct for golden light and fewer tourists.
🚇
Transport
Navigo weekly pass (£22) covers unlimited metro and RER. Buy at any station with a photo.
🗣️
Language
Learn bonjour, merci and excusez-moi — Parisians appreciate the effort even if they reply in English.
🖼️
Free Museums
Musée Carnavalet (Paris history), Musée de la Chasse, many municipal museums — free entry.
// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Louvre — What to Actually See
Don't attempt the whole thing. Focus: Denon Wing (Winged Victory, Venus de Milo, the Mona Lisa corridor), then Richelieu for Dutch masters. Book timed entry to skip the pyramid queue.
02
Musée d'Orsay — Impressionism
The railway station conversion with the world's greatest Impressionist collection — Monet, Renoir, Degas, van Gogh. Less crowded than the Louvre and more immediately beautiful.
03
Montmartre at Dusk
The hilltop village — Place du Tertre, Sacré-Cœur for the panoramic view, then descend to Abbesses for dinner in the best street in the neighbourhood (Rue Lepic).
04
Seine River Picnic Near Île de la Cité
Wine, cheese and a baguette from the Marché d'Aligre (Thu–Sun), eaten on the riverbank near Pont Neuf — the definitive Paris afternoon.
05
Marais — Medieval Paris & Jewish Quarter
The most neighbourhood-feeling central district — 14th-century lanes, the Jewish bakeries of Rue des Rosiers, the Centre Pompidou and the Place des Vosges (Paris's most beautiful square).
06
Covered Passages, 2nd Arrondissement
The 19th-century glass-roofed arcades — Galerie Vivienne, Passage des Panoramas — the most atmospheric free experience in Paris.
// Areas & Neighbourhoods

Where to base yourself

Marais (3rd & 4th)
Medieval streets, Jewish quarter, LGBTQ+
The most neighbourhood-feeling central area — excellent for walking, eating and the best of historic Paris without the tourist chaos.
St-Germain-des-Prés (6th)
Cafes, bookshops, Luxembourg Gardens
The intellectual Left Bank — Café de Flore, Shakespeare & Co., the Luxembourg Gardens and the best boulangeries in Paris.
Canal Saint-Martin (10th)
Local, brunch, boutiques
The most genuinely Parisian neighbourhood — iron footbridges over the canal, trendy restaurants and the Paris that actual Parisians inhabit.

// When to Go

Season guide

Apr–Jun: The classic Paris experience — long evenings, chestnut blossom and the city in full bloom. Sep–Oct: Excellent — summer crowds gone, golden autumn light. Jul–Aug: Hot, extremely crowded and expensive despite many Parisians leaving. Not the best time. Nov–Mar: Cold but dramatically less crowded — the Louvre without queues in January is a revelation.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🗣️

Even bonjour and merci are appreciated. Parisians are not rude — they simply prefer minimal social formality with strangers.

🎫

Book the Louvre, Orsay and Eiffel Tower online — long queues without pre-booking are unavoidable in peak season.

🚇

Paris Visite or Navigo weekly pass covers unlimited metro and RER. Buy at the airport on arrival.

🚲

Vélib' bike share is the best way to see Paris — stands throughout the city, cheap daily passes.

🆓

Many of Paris's best experiences are free: the Louvre courtyard, all church interiors, the covered passages, the Seine, the Marais streets.
