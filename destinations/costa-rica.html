// Central America

Costa Rica

6% of world biodiversity in one country — volcanoes, cloud forest and extraordinary wildlife.

💰 £40–£80/day 🗓️ Best: Dec–Apr 🛂 No visa (UK/EU/US, 90 days) 💬 Spanish (English in tourist areas)

Sloth in the cloud, volcano at night

Costa Rica has 26% of its territory protected as national park — the most of any country on earth. The result is extraordinary wildlife density: sloths, scarlet macaws, resplendent quetzals and four species of sea turtle all within a country the size of Wales.

The infrastructure is the best in Central America. The lodges are excellent. The food is good. And the commitment to conservation is genuine, not marketing. It is more expensive than its neighbours, but it delivers on every promise.

// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Arenal Volcano & Hot Springs
Hike the 1968 lava fields and soak in the volcanic hot spring pools at night below the active cone.
02
Monteverde Cloud Forest Reserve
Hanging bridges through the canopy — quetzals, toucans, howler monkeys and tree frogs in the mist.
03
Manuel Antonio National Park
The most-visited park — white-faced capuchins, sloths, squirrel monkeys and a white sand beach inside the park boundary.
04
Tortuguero Turtle Nesting (Jul–Oct)
Green sea turtles nesting on the Caribbean coast — the largest nesting site in the western hemisphere. Accessible only by boat.
05
Pacific Surf — Nosara & Tamarindo
Consistent beach breaks for all levels. Nosara for beginners (Witch's Rock surf camp); Playa Hermosa near Jacó for advanced.
06
Corcovado National Park
The most biodiverse place on earth per unit area — jaguars, tapirs, scarlet macaws. Remote and requiring advance planning.

// When to Go

Season guide

Dec–Apr: Dry season — Pacific coast at its best. Jul–Oct: Caribbean coast dry season — turtle nesting peaks. May–Nov: Green season — heavy afternoon rain, major price drops.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🚗

Car hire is strongly recommended — the ecolodges are spread across difficult terrain.

🚙

Rent a 4WD — many lodge roads are unpaved and impassable in rain with a standard car.

🏕️

Book popular lodges 2–3 months ahead for the dry season. The best wildlife lodges fill fast.

🕐

Costa Rican 'Tico time' is real — buses and services run on a flexible schedule. Build in flexibility.

🦎

Always check shoes before putting them on and avoid reaching into crevices in jungle areas.
