// Oceania · New Zealand

Queenstown & the South Island

The adventure capital of the world and the most dramatic fjord landscapes on earth.

💰 £55–£90/day 🗓️ Best: Dec–Mar & Jun–Oct (ski) 🛂 NZeTA required (NZ$23) 💬 English, Māori

Adventure capital, fjord country

New Zealand's South Island is where the country's reputation for extraordinary landscape is most earned. Queenstown is the world's adventure capital — bungee jumping, skydiving, jet boating and world-class skiing. Milford Sound has 300m vertical walls dropping directly into fjord water.

The Great Walks — Milford Track, Routeburn Track, Kepler Track — are among the world's finest multi-day treks with Department of Conservation huts throughout. The drive from Christchurch to Queenstown through the Mackenzie Basin is one of the great road trips.

// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Milford Sound Cruise
The fjord with 300m walls — waterfalls cascade from every cliff and seals bask on the rocks. Take the early morning cruise for the fewest tourists and best light.
02
Queenstown Bungee & Adventure
The original AJ Hackett bungee above the Kawarau River — also skydiving, jet boating and paragliding from the Remarkables.
03
Remarkables Ski Field
Above Queenstown with views over Lake Wakatipu — June to October. More accessible than Coronet Peak with excellent intermediate terrain.
04
Hobbiton Movie Set Tour, Matamata
The Shire from Lord of the Rings — the most detailed film set ever built, still maintained exactly as it appeared in the films.
05
Milford Track — 4 Days
The world's most famous multi-day walk — huts every night, Sutherland Falls (580m), the MacKinnon Pass views. Book months ahead.
06
Lake Tekapo & Aoraki/Mt Cook
The turquoise glacial lake with the Church of the Good Shepherd and the highest peak in Australasia rising above the Mackenzie Basin.

// When to Go

Season guide

Dec–Mar: Summer hiking and South Island scenery at its best. Jun–Oct: Ski season in the Southern Alps. Sep–Nov: Spring — uncrowded with wildflowers. Apr–May: Autumn colours, fewer tourists.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🏕️

Great Walk DOC permits book out months ahead — especially Milford Track. Book the day permits release in June.

🚗

Driving the South Island is the best way to experience it — the Christchurch–Queenstown route is extraordinary.

🛂

NZeTA (NZ$23) required for most nationalities — apply at nzeta.govt.nz before travel.

🌿

Leave No Trace in DOC areas — New Zealand's conservation areas are extraordinary and fragile.

🦺

Working Holiday Visa available to most under-30 Western nationals — apply at immigration.govt.nz.
