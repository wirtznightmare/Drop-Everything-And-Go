// Central America

Guatemala

Three volcanoes, a UNESCO lake and Mayan jungle ruins — Central America's most beautiful country.

💰 £20–£40/day 🗓️ Best: Nov–Apr 🛂 No visa (UK/EU/US) 💬 Spanish, 23 Mayan languages

Volcanoes, lake and ancient jungle

Guatemala is the most visually stunning country in Central America. Antigua sits in a valley surrounded by three active volcanoes, its baroque ruins and cobblestone streets earning UNESCO World Heritage status. Lake Atitlán has three volcanoes rising from the water and Mayan villages on every shore.

Tikal in the north is one of the great archaeological sites of the ancient world — a Mayan city buried in jungle with temples taller than the forest canopy. Guatemala is small, affordable and extraordinarily dense with beauty.

// Quick Facts

💰
Daily Budget
£20–40. One of the most affordable countries in Central America.
🎒
Chicken Buses
Repurposed US school buses — the authentic and very cheap way between towns. Slow and unforgettable.
🏫
Spanish School
Antigua has world-class Spanish schools — £120–150/week for 4 hours/day one-to-one lessons.
🌋
Volcanoes
Acatenango overnight hike gives lava views from Fuego — one of the great Central American experiences.
💱
Currency
Guatemalan Quetzal (GTQ). ATMs can be unreliable in rural areas — carry cash.
// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Antigua — Colonial City & Spanish School
The most beautiful colonial city in Central America — baroque ruins, cobblestones under a volcano. World-class Spanish language schools for £120/week.
02
Lake Atitlán Village Hopping
The most beautiful lake in Central America — boat between San Juan (artisan cooperative), San Marcos (yoga) and Santiago (traditional Mayan culture).
03
Tikal Jungle Ruins at Sunrise
The Mayan city in the jungle — climb Temple IV at dawn as temples rise above the canopy and howler monkeys begin screaming. One of the great archaeological experiences.
04
Acatenango Volcano Overnight Hike
The overnight ascent to watch Fuego erupting at night — lava glowing against the dark sky from the crater of the adjacent volcano.
05
Chichicastenango Market
The largest indigenous market in Central America on Thursdays and Sundays — Mayan spiritual rituals on the church steps, textiles and traditional medicine.
06
Semuc Champey Turquoise Pools
The natural limestone bridge over the Cahabón River with turquoise pools — one of the most beautiful natural sites in Central America, on a long bumpy road.

// When to Go

Season guide

Nov–Apr: Dry season — best for Tikal and the highlands. Dec–Jan: Peak Antigua season, Christmas processions spectacular. May–Oct: Rainy season — heavy afternoon rain, mornings usually clear.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🚌

Chicken buses are very cheap and authentic. Registered shuttles are safer for Tikal and remote areas.

🏫

Antigua Spanish schools are genuinely world-class — 4 hours/day one-to-one for £120–150/week.

🏔️

Altitude sickness possible around Antigua (1,530m) — take the first day slowly if arriving from sea level.

💵

Carry enough Quetzales for multi-day trips — ATMs unreliable in rural areas.

🛡️

Normal precautions in Guatemala City and on tourist routes. Registered shuttles recommended for Tikal.
