Atacama & Patagonia
Chile

The driest desert on earth in the north. The wildest mountains on earth in the south.

South America · Chile

Why visit Chile?

Chile runs 4,300km from north to south and the geographic range is extraordinary. The Atacama Desert in the north is the driest non-polar place on earth, at altitude under skies so clear that the world's major observatories are built here. Patagonia in the south contains the most dramatic accessible mountain landscape on earth.

Torres del Paine's W Circuit is one of the world's great treks. The Carretera Austral is one of the world's great road trips. Between them, Chile offers two completely different versions of extraordinary.

Who It's Perfect For
🔭

Stargazers

The Atacama has the clearest skies on earth — the world's major observatories are built here and public stargazing tours are extraordinary.

🥾

Trekkers

Torres del Paine's W Circuit, the Carretera Austral hiking and the Villarrica volcano crater walk are among South America's best.

🏎️

Road Trip Lovers

The Carretera Austral (1,200km unpaved southern highway) and the Icefields Parkway approach on the Patagonian roads are two of the world's great drives.

The Highlights

Things to Do & See

01
Torres del Paine W Circuit — 5 days through the granite towers, French Valley glacier and Grey Glacier
02
El Tatio geysers at 4am — 4,500m altitude, 80+ geysers erupting in the pre-dawn cold
03
Atacama Valley of the Moon at sunset — Mars-like salt formations and dunes in extraordinary light
04
Atacama stargazing observatory — professional telescopes and an astronomer guide under the world's clearest skies
05
Carretera Austral road trip — the 1,200km unpaved southern highway through virgin forest and glaciers
06
Easter Island (Rapa Nui) — 887 moai statues on the world's most isolated inhabited island
07
Lake District — Puerto Varas, Osorno volcano and the crossing to Bariloche by lake and road
08
Santiago food scene — Barrio Italia restaurants and Bellavista nightlife are South America's most sophisticated
