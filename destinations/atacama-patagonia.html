// South America · Chile

Atacama & Patagonia

The driest desert on earth in the north. The wildest mountains on earth in the south.

💰 £35–£65/day 🗓️ Best: Nov–Mar (Patagonia) & May–Sep (Atacama) 🛂 No visa (UK/EU/US, 90 days) 💬 Spanish

Desert stars, Patagonian towers

Chile runs 4,300km from north to south — the geographic range is extraordinary. The Atacama Desert in the north is the driest non-polar place on earth, at altitude under skies so clear that the world's major observatories are built here. Patagonia in the south contains the most dramatic accessible mountain landscape on earth.

Torres del Paine's W Circuit is one of the world's great treks. The Carretera Austral is one of the world's great road trips. Between them, Chile offers two completely different versions of extraordinary.

// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Torres del Paine W Circuit — 5 Days
The granite towers at dawn, the French Valley glacier and Grey Glacier — one of the world's great multi-day treks. Book lodges months ahead.
02
Atacama — Valley of the Moon at Sunset
The Mars-like salt formations and dunes at sunset — completely surreal in the world's most arid landscape.
03
El Tatio Geysers at 4am
4,500m altitude, 80+ geysers erupting in the pre-dawn cold — the largest geyser field in the Southern Hemisphere.
04
Atacama Stargazing Observatory
The world's clearest skies with professional telescopes and an astronomer guide — the Southern Cross, the Milky Way core and nebulae visible to the naked eye.
05
Carretera Austral Road Trip
The 1,200km unpaved southern highway through virgin forest, hanging glaciers and empty wilderness — one of the world's great drives.
06
Easter Island — the Moai Statues
The most isolated inhabited island on earth — 887 stone statues built by the Rapa Nui, 5 hours by air from Santiago.
// Areas & Neighbourhoods

Where to base yourself

San Pedro de Atacama
Desert, stargazing, geysers
The base town at 2,400m — day trips to salt flats, Valley of the Moon, El Tatio geysers and the night sky observatories.
Puerto Natales & Torres del Paine
Trekking, glaciers, wilderness
The gateway to Torres del Paine — base here. Book all accommodation months ahead.
Santiago & Valparaíso
City base, wine country
Chile's sophisticated capital and the chaotic coloured hillside port city — both worth 2–3 days.

// When to Go

Season guide

Nov–Mar: Patagonia summer — W Circuit season. May–Sep (Atacama): Winter nights are even clearer for stargazing. Apr–May: Autumn — fewer visitors, beautiful in Patagonia.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🏕️

Torres del Paine accommodation books out from October onwards — book at fantastico.cl immediately.

🌬️

Patagonian wind is extreme. A proper windproof jacket — not a fleece — is the difference between miserable and magnificent.

🔭

Altitude acclimatisation in the Atacama — rest the first day in San Pedro before any excursions.

🚗

Carretera Austral requires 4WD and careful fuel planning — fill up every time you see a station.

🍷

Chilean wine (Carménère, Sauvignon Blanc) is world-class value in-country.
