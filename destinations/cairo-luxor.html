Egypt — Cairo & Luxor
Egypt

The Pyramids are exactly as extraordinary as everyone says — and the Valley of the Kings is the bonus.

Middle East & North Africa

Why visit Egypt?

Standing in front of the Pyramids of Giza is one of the few travel experiences that genuinely lives up to the build-up. They are bigger than you expect. Older than you can process. And they exist in a city of 22 million people in a way that is completely disorienting. The Sphinx in the foreground, the Cairo skyline behind — nothing prepares you for the scale.

Upper Egypt — Luxor, Aswan, Abu Simbel — is where you come after the Pyramids. The Valley of the Kings contains 63 royal tombs. Karnak Temple is the largest religious structure ever built. A Nile cruise is one of the world's great slow journeys.

Who It's Perfect For
🏛️

History Lovers

More UNESCO World Heritage Sites than any country on earth except Italy. The Pyramids, Valley of the Kings, Karnak, Abu Simbel — human civilisation at its peak.

📸

Photographers

The Pyramids at sunrise, Karnak's Hypostyle Hall, the golden light on the Nile — Egypt is one of the world's great photography destinations.

🚢

Slow Travellers

A Nile cruise from Luxor to Aswan — three days between ancient temples on either bank — is one of the world's most civilised ways to travel.

The Highlights

Things to Do & See

01
Pyramids of Giza at sunrise — hire a horse on the plateau for the panoramic view, enter the Great Pyramid interior
02
Valley of the Kings in Luxor — the standard ticket covers three tombs; Ramesses VI is the finest
03
Karnak Temple at 6am opening — 134 massive columns in the Hypostyle Hall with hieroglyphics in the early light
04
Nile cruise from Luxor to Aswan — 3 nights on a modern ship between ancient temples
05
Abu Simbel — the 3,000-year-old temples literally moved in 1968 to avoid rising waters
06
Egyptian Museum — the golden death mask of Tutankhamun, the Royal Mummies room, 120,000 objects
07
Sunset felucca cruise in Aswan — traditional sailboat past Elephantine Island on the most beautiful stretch of the Nile
08
Karnak Sound and Light Show — the Hypostyle Hall illuminated at night
Where to Stay

Base Yourself in Egypt — Cairo & Luxor

🏙️

Cairo & Giza

Pyramids, Egyptian Museum, city

The capital with the pyramids on its western edge. Stay in Zamalek for elegance or near the pyramids for early access.

🏛️

Luxor

Valley of Kings, temples, archaeology

The open-air museum of Upper Egypt — 2–3 nights minimum to see everything.

🌊

Aswan & Abu Simbel

Nubian culture, Nile views, Abu Simbel

The most beautiful city in Egypt — 3-hour drive south to the relocated temples.
