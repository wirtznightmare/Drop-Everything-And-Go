// Middle East & North Africa

Egypt — Cairo & Luxor

The Pyramids are exactly as extraordinary as everyone says. Everything else in Egypt is the bonus.

💰 £25–£55/day 🗓️ Best: Oct–Apr 🛂 Visa on arrival $25 or e-Visa online 💬 Arabic, English in tourist areas

4,500 years still standing

Standing in front of the Pyramids of Giza is one of the few travel experiences that genuinely lives up to the build-up. They are bigger than you expect. Older than you can process. And they exist in a city of 22 million people in a way that is completely disorienting. The Sphinx in the foreground, the Cairo skyline behind — nothing prepares you for the scale.

Upper Egypt — Luxor, Aswan, Abu Simbel — is where you come after the Pyramids. The Valley of the Kings contains the tombs of 63 pharaohs. Karnak Temple is the largest religious structure ever built. A Nile cruise from Luxor to Aswan is one of the world's great slow journeys.

// Quick Facts

💰
Daily Budget
£25–55. Temple entry fees are significant (£6–18 each) — budget properly.
🌡️
Climate
Oct–Apr comfortable (20–28°C). May–Sep extreme heat in Upper Egypt (40–48°C).
🚂
Nile Cruise
Luxor to Aswan — 3 nights on a modern cruise ship or traditional dahabiya. Book in advance Nov–Feb.
💵
Tipping
Baksheesh is expected everywhere. Carry small EGP notes in an accessible pocket.
🎟️
Great Pyramid
The interior requires a separate ticket (500 EGP) — buy online, numbers are limited daily.
// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Pyramids of Giza at Sunrise
The last remaining Wonder of the Ancient World — hire a horse on the plateau for the panoramic view, enter the Great Pyramid interior and watch the city wake up around an ancient miracle.
02
Valley of the Kings, Luxor
The hidden tombs of 63 pharaohs in the Theban cliffs — the standard ticket covers three tombs. Ramesses VI is extraordinary; Tutankhamun's is underwhelming but famous.
03
Karnak Temple at First Light
The largest religious structure ever built — 134 massive stone columns in the Hypostyle Hall. Visit at opening (6am) when the early light catches the hieroglyphics.
04
Nile Cruise — Luxor to Aswan
3 nights on a classic cruise between ancient temples on either bank — the most atmospheric way to travel through Upper Egypt.
05
Abu Simbel — 3,000-Year-Old Relocated Temples
The cliff-face temples of Ramesses II, literally moved in 1968 to avoid rising waters — the engineering story is as extraordinary as the temples themselves.
06
Egyptian Museum — Tutankhamun's Treasures
The world's greatest collection of ancient Egyptian artefacts — the golden death mask, the Royal Mummies room and 120,000 objects under one roof.
// Areas & Neighbourhoods

Where to base yourself

Cairo & Giza
Pyramids, museum, city
The capital with the pyramids on its western edge — stay in Zamalek (elegant island district) or near the pyramids. Allow 2–3 days.
Luxor
Valley of Kings, temples
The open-air museum of Upper Egypt — Karnak, the Valley of the Kings and the Valley of the Queens all within easy reach. Stay 2–3 nights minimum.
Aswan & Abu Simbel
Nubian culture, Nile views
The most relaxed and beautiful city in Egypt — the Nubian culture is distinct and Abu Simbel is a 3-hour drive or 45-min flight south.

// When to Go

Season guide

Oct–Apr: The entire winter is comfortable across Egypt. Nov–Feb: Peak season — book Nile cruises in advance. Mar–Apr: Excellent shoulder season. May–Sep: Extremely hot in Upper Egypt (40–48°C). Only the Mediterranean coast and Sinai are manageable in summer.

// Need to Know

Before you go

💵

Tipping (baksheesh) is expected everywhere — have small denomination EGP notes ready for temple guards, helpers and anyone who assists.

🐫

The touts around the Pyramids are extremely persistent. Buy camel and horse rides through your hotel in advance — lower price and eliminates most hassle.

🎟️

Book the Great Pyramid interior online — tickets are limited daily and sell out. The standard Giza ticket does not include the interior.

🚂

The overnight sleeper train from Cairo to Luxor/Aswan (Watania/Abela Egypt) is comfortable and an experience in itself. Book in advance.

👔

Dress modestly outside beach resorts — covered shoulders and knees in cities and archaeological sites.
