Amsterdam
Netherlands

17th-century canals, world-class museums and Europe's most cycling-friendly capital.

Europe · Netherlands

Why visit Amsterdam?

Amsterdam is one of Europe's most beautifully preserved cities — the entire canal ring is UNESCO-listed, the 17th-century merchant houses lean over the water at improbable angles, and the whole city is designed around bicycles. The Rijksmuseum houses the greatest Dutch Golden Age collection in existence, and the Van Gogh Museum is genuinely moving. The Anne Frank House is essential.

Beyond the obvious: the Jordaan neighbourhood's brown cafes, the NDSM Wharf creative district across the IJ and a day trip to Giethoorn (the village with no roads) are the parts worth discovering.

Who It's Perfect For
🖼️

Art Lovers

Rijksmuseum (Dutch Golden Age), Van Gogh Museum, Stedelijk (modern art) and Anne Frank House make Amsterdam one of Europe's greatest art cities.

🚲

Active Travellers

The world's best cycling city — flat, safe dedicated lanes, and a bike gives you access to the Jordaan's smallest canals that no tourist bus reaches.

🌷

Romantics & Couples

Canal boat at dusk, tulip season at Keukenhof, the Jordaan neighbourhood at night — Amsterdam delivers the romantic European city break completely.

The Highlights

Things to Do & See

01
Book the Rijksmuseum online — focus on the Night Watch and Vermeer's complete works
02
Book Anne Frank House months ahead — it genuinely sells out and there is no walk-up option
03
Rent a bike and cycle the Jordaan canals at dawn — Brouwersgracht is the most beautiful
04
Visit Keukenhof during tulip season (late March–mid May) — 7 million bulbs in bloom
05
Book the Van Gogh Museum timed entry — the emotional arc of his work is extraordinary
06
Take the free IJ Ferry to Amsterdam Noord — NDSM Wharf and EYE film museum
07
Try raw herring (haring) from a street stall — the Dutch way is tail in hand, head back
08
Day trip to Giethoorn — the village with no roads, 90 minutes by train and bus
