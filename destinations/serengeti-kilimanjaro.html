// Africa · Tanzania

Serengeti & Kilimanjaro

The Great Migration, the Ngorongoro Crater and the roof of Africa at 5,895m.

💰 £50–£150/day 🗓️ Best: Jun–Oct 🛂 e-Visa required (immigration.go.tz) 💬 Swahili, English

The world's greatest wildlife spectacle

The Great Migration — 1.5 million wildebeest in continuous circular movement between Tanzania and Kenya, with the Mara River crossings between July and October — is the most extraordinary wildlife event on earth. The Ngorongoro Crater adds a complete ecosystem in a volcanic caldera.

Kilimanjaro stands 5,895m above the surrounding plain — Africa's highest peak, accessible without technical climbing on the 7-day Machame Route. The combination of safari and summit in one Tanzania trip is uniquely compelling.

// Quick Facts

💰
Daily Budget
Budget camping safaris from £80/day. Mid-range lodges £150–300. Luxury camps £500+.
🦁
Migration
Mara River crossings July–October. Calving season January–February.
🏔️
Kilimanjaro
Machame Route (7 days) has the best acclimatisation profile. Nearly half of attempts get altitude sickness.
💉
Vaccinations
Yellow Fever certificate may be required. Malaria prophylaxis essential.
✈️
Airport
Fly into Kilimanjaro International (JRO) or Julius Nyerere (DAR).
// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Serengeti Great Migration Safari
1.5 million wildebeest — the Mara River crossings with crocodiles waiting at every ford are the most dramatic wildlife spectacle on earth.
02
Ngorongoro Crater Full Day Drive
600 square kilometres inside a volcanic caldera — every East African large mammal in one day, including the rare black rhino.
03
Kilimanjaro Summit — Uhuru Peak
The highest point in Africa — no technical equipment needed but the Machame Route (7 days) gives the best acclimatisation.
04
Zanzibar Beach Extension
35-minute flight from Dar — Stone Town history and the north coast (Nungwi) beaches. The perfect post-safari week.
05
Tarangire National Park
Dry season elephant concentration at the river — thousands in one valley with baobab trees everywhere.
06
Selous/Nyerere Walking Safari
The largest protected area in Africa — walking safaris possible here (banned in northern parks), fewer tourist vehicles.

// When to Go

Season guide

Jun–Oct: Dry season — peak Migration and best wildlife viewing. Jan–Feb: Calving season. Mar–May: Long rains — rates lower, some lodges close.

// Need to Know

Before you go

💉

Yellow Fever certificate required for Tanzania. Malaria prophylaxis essential.

🦁

Safari quality varies enormously — research operators carefully and book with licensed guides.

🏔️

Kilimanjaro acclimatisation is serious. Choose 7-day Machame or Lemosho routes.

✈️

Combine northern circuit with Zanzibar — 35-minute flight and one of the great itineraries.

💵

Always tip guides and camp staff generously — wages are low and tips make a real difference.
