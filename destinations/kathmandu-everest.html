Nepal — Kathmandu & Everest
Nepal

Eight of the world's ten highest peaks — and valleys that match them for beauty.

South Asia · Nepal

Why trek Nepal?

Nepal is where you come for the mountains — and nothing else on earth quite prepares you for the Himalayas viewed from the trail. Everest Base Camp (5,364m), the Annapurna Circuit and the Langtang Valley are three of the world's great long-distance treks, and Nepal's teahouse system means you can walk for weeks carrying only a daypack.

Kathmandu is extraordinary in its own right — three UNESCO Durbar Squares, Boudhanath Stupa and Pashupatinath temple all within a short taxi ride. Pokhara, the gateway to Annapurna, is one of Asia's most beautiful lakeside towns.

Who It's Perfect For
🏔️

Trekkers

The world's greatest trekking — Everest Base Camp, Annapurna Circuit, Annapurna Base Camp, Langtang. The teahouse system makes it accessible for any fitness level.

⛩️

Culture Seekers

Three UNESCO Durbar Squares, Boudhanath Stupa, Pashupatinath temple, Buddhist monasteries in the hills — Nepal's culture is extraordinary.

🦏

Wildlife Enthusiasts

Chitwan National Park for one-horned rhinos, Bengal tigers and elephant grasslands — a complete contrast to the mountains.

The Highlights

Things to Do & See

01
Everest Base Camp Trek — 16 days from Lukla to 5,364m through the Khumbu Valley. Teahouses every night.
02
Boudhanath Stupa morning circumambulation — one of the world's largest stupas with monks and pilgrims at dawn
03
Kathmandu Durbar Square at 6am — medieval royal squares with temples dating to the 12th century
04
Pashupatinath Temple — the most important Hindu temple in Nepal, best in early morning and at evening puja
05
Pokhara and the Annapurna reflection — Fewa Lake at dawn with Fishtail and Annapurna South reflected
06
Annapurna Poon Hill trek — 4-day alternative to the full circuit with the famous sunrise mountain view
07
Chitwan National Park — rhino and tiger spotting in the Terai lowlands, completely different to the mountains
08
Day hike above Nagarkot for the greatest Himalayan sunrise panorama accessible from Kathmandu

Insider Knowledge

What Most Guides Don't Tell You About Nepal — Kathmandu & Everest

  • →

    All trekkers need a TIMS card and national park permit — buy at the Nepal Tourism Board in Thamel, not online

  • →

    Altitude sickness kills people who ascend too fast — never gain more than 300–500m per day above 3,000m

  • →

    Hire a licensed guide and porter — it directly supports the Sherpa communities and significantly improves safety

  • →

    Everything for trekking can be rented in Thamel — sleeping bags, down jackets, poles. Don't buy at home.

  • →

    The best gear rental in Asia is in Kathmandu — rent rather than buy for a significantly lower cost
