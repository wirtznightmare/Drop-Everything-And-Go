// South Asia

Nepal — Kathmandu & Everest

Eight of the world's ten highest peaks — and valleys that match them for beauty.

💰 £20–£45/day 🗓️ Best: Oct–Nov & Mar–Apr 🛂 Visa on arrival ($30 for 15 days) 💬 Nepali, English on trekking routes

The world's highest trekking

Nepal is where you come for the mountains — and nothing else on earth quite prepares you for the Himalayas viewed from the trail. Everest Base Camp (5,364m), the Annapurna Circuit and the Langtang Valley are three of the world's great long-distance treks, and Nepal's teahouse system means you can walk for weeks carrying only a daypack.

Kathmandu is extraordinary in its own right — three UNESCO Durbar Squares, Boudhanath Stupa and Pashupatinath temple all within a short taxi ride of each other. Pokhara, the gateway to Annapurna, is one of Asia's most beautiful lakeside towns.

// Quick Facts

💰
Daily Budget
Kathmandu from £20/day. Trekking adds £15–25 for teahouse bed and board.
🎫
Permits
TIMS card (£15) + national park permit (£20–30) for all major treks. Buy at TAAN office, Kathmandu.
🏔️
Altitude
Acclimatise properly — never ascend more than 300–500m per day above 3,000m.
✈️
Flights
Tribhuvan Airport, Kathmandu. Internal flights to Lukla for EBC (15 min, spectacular).
🎒
Kit
Everything available to rent in Thamel, Kathmandu — sleeping bag, down jacket, poles.
// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Everest Base Camp Trek — 16 Days
The most famous trek in the world — from Lukla through the Khumbu Valley to 5,364m. Teahouses every night, no camping required, spectacular throughout.
02
Kathmandu Durbar Square at Dawn
The medieval royal square with temples dating to the 12th century — arrive at 6am before vendors set up for the most atmospheric Kathmandu experience.
03
Boudhanath Stupa Morning Circumambulation
One of the world's largest Buddhist stupas — the early morning circular walk with monks and pilgrims is deeply atmospheric and completely free.
04
Annapurna Circuit or Short Trek
The classic high-altitude circuit (12–21 days) or a 3–5 day Ghorepani-Poon Hill alternative with Annapurna sunrise views without the full commitment.
05
Pokhara — Fewa Lake & Mountain Views
The lakeside gateway town with Fishtail and Annapurna South reflected in the lake at dawn — the most beautiful mountain view accessible without trekking.
06
Chitwan National Park Safari
One-horned rhinos, Bengal tigers and elephant grasslands in the Terai lowlands — a complete contrast to the mountains and excellent wildlife.
// Areas & Neighbourhoods

Where to base yourself

Kathmandu
Culture, temples, trekking prep
The chaotic capital — Thamel neighbourhood for gear rental and guesthouses, the old city for Durbar Squares and the stupas. Allow 2–3 days before trekking.
Pokhara
Trekking base, Annapurna
The lakeside gateway — relaxed, beautiful and the best base for Annapurna treks and shorter hill walks. Far more peaceful than Kathmandu.
Namche Bazaar — EBC Route
Sherpa culture, acclimatisation
The Sherpa capital at 3,440m — the mandatory acclimatisation stop on Everest trail, with a Saturday market and mountain views.

// When to Go

Season guide

Oct–Nov: Post-monsoon — the best visibility of the year. Himalayan views at their most dramatic and crystal clear. Best trekking window. Mar–Apr: Pre-monsoon — rhododendrons in bloom, warm and beautiful. Also excellent. Dec–Feb: Cold at altitude but fine in Kathmandu and the lowlands. Very quiet trails. Jun–Sep: Monsoon — trails wet and muddy. The Mustang region is in a rain shadow and trekkable.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🎫

All trekkers need a TIMS card and ACAP/SAGAP permit. Buy at the Nepal Tourism Board office in Thamel — not from touts or online resellers.

🏔️

Altitude sickness kills people who ascend too fast. Follow the 'climb high, sleep low' principle. If you have symptoms, descend immediately.

👤

Hire a licensed guide and porter for any serious trek — it directly supports the local Sherpa communities and significantly improves safety.

💰

Teahouses on major routes provide bed and board at fixed prices — typically £5–8 for a room and £3–5 per meal. No need to carry camping equipment.

🎒

Rent gear in Thamel rather than buying at home — sleeping bags, down jackets and poles available at a fraction of Western prices.
