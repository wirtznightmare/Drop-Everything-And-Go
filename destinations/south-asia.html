// South Asia

South
Asia

The subcontinent that overwhelms every sense — and then keeps you coming back.

South Asia is the most intense travel experience most people will ever have. India alone contains more history, culture, cuisine and landscape variety than most continents. Nepal sits in the shadow of the highest mountains on earth. Sri Lanka packs extraordinary beaches, ancient ruins and wildlife into an island the size of Ireland. The Maldives floats in the Indian Ocean like a geography lesson about paradise. Start with India if you can handle the scale of it — it rewards the effort profoundly. Nepal for trekking — even if you don't do Everest Base Camp, the Annapurna circuit and Langtang valley are extraordinary. Sri Lanka for a two-week circuit. The Maldives for the beach holiday of your life.

// When to Go

Season guide

India (north): October–March for the Golden Triangle (Delhi, Agra, Jaipur) — pleasant temperatures. April–June is extreme heat. Monsoon July–September makes travel difficult. Goa: November–February is peak season — dry and warm. Kerala: December–March is ideal. Nepal trekking: October–November (post-monsoon clarity, best visibility) and March–April (pre-monsoon, rhododendrons in bloom). Sri Lanka: December–March for the west coast; May–September for the east coast. Maldives: November–April (dry season) is ideal; May–October brings rain but significant price drops.

// Need to Know

Before you go

ℹ️

India is not for the faint-hearted as a first international trip — the sensory overload is real. Come after a few other trips, or come with someone who's been before.

ℹ️

A full Nepal trekking permit costs $30 and TIMS card $20. Book through a registered agency to ensure your guide and porter are insured.

ℹ️

Indian e-Visa is available for most nationalities — apply 4–7 days before travel on the official Indian government site.

ℹ️

Altitude sickness above 3,000m in Nepal is serious. Ascend gradually, follow the acclimatisation schedule and don't skip rest days at Namche Bazaar.

ℹ️

The Maldives is one of the few countries where budget and luxury genuinely co-exist — guesthouse islands (Maafushi, Dhigurah) offer the same reefs as the overwater villas for 10% of the price.
