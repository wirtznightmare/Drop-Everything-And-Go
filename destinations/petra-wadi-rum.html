// Middle East · Jordan

Petra & Wadi Rum

Petra at dawn. Wadi Rum at night. The Dead Sea in between. Jordan earns every superlative.

💰 £35–£65/day 🗓️ Best: Mar–May & Sep–Nov 🛂 Jordan Pass recommended (£55 includes visa + Petra entry) 💬 Arabic, English in tourist areas

Rose-red city, crimson desert

Walking through the Siq at dawn — the 1.2km sandstone canyon narrowing until the Treasury appears in the first morning light — is one of those travel moments that stays with you for life. Petra is genuinely one of the greatest archaeological sites on earth and the only criticism anyone can make is that one day is not enough.

Wadi Rum adds the second dimension: the red desert valley where Lawrence of Arabia was filmed looks like Mars and offers the best stargazing anywhere accessible without specialist equipment. Jordan itself is one of the most welcoming countries in the Middle East — Jordanians go out of their way for visitors.

// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Petra Treasury at Dawn
Arrive at gate opening (6am) to walk the Siq in silence before the tour groups. The Treasury in early light — the most photographed facade in the Middle East — is genuinely overwhelming.
02
Petra — Beyond the Treasury
Most visitors stop at the Treasury. The Royal Tombs, the Colonnaded Street, the Great Temple and the Monastery (45 more minutes up 800 steps) are equally extraordinary.
03
Wadi Rum Overnight Desert Camp
Jeep tour through the red valley at sunset, traditional Bedouin dinner and sleeping under the densest stars visible from any accessible place in the Middle East.
04
Dead Sea Float
Floating effortlessly in the saltiest large body of water on earth at the lowest point on the planet — genuinely disorienting, genuinely fun.
05
Petra by Night
Monday, Wednesday and Thursday only — Petra lit by 1,500 candles. Walk the Siq in complete silence through the candlelit canyon to the lit Treasury.
06
Jerash Roman Ruins
One of the best-preserved Roman cities outside Italy — the Oval Forum, the Cardo Maximus and Hadrian's Arch. 45 minutes north of Amman.
// Areas & Neighbourhoods

Where to base yourself

Wadi Musa (Petra Town)
Petra access, Nabataean history
The town directly adjacent to Petra — stay here for 2+ days to see the full site. Several excellent guesthouses with rooftop Treasury views.
Wadi Rum
Desert camping, Bedouin culture
The desert valley — stay in a Bedouin camp for the full experience. 1 hour south of Petra and worth the extra night.
Aqaba & Dead Sea
Red Sea diving, Dead Sea float
Jordan's Red Sea town has excellent snorkelling and diving. The Dead Sea is best as a day trip from Amman or en route north.

// When to Go

Season guide

Mar–May: Perfect spring weather — warm days (20–28°C), wildflowers in Wadi Rum. The best window overall. Sep–Nov: Warm and very pleasant, fewer tourists than spring. Dec–Feb: Cool to cold — Petra gets cold at altitude (1,000m). Wadi Rum can freeze at night but is beautiful in winter light. Jun–Aug: Very hot (35–40°C). Petra is manageable in early morning only.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🎫

Buy the Jordan Pass online before arriving at myjordanpass.com — includes visa, 2-day Petra entry and 40+ sites. Much better value than paying separately.

⏳

Petra requires at least 2 days to see properly — the Monastery alone takes 2 hours from the main site. Most tourists dramatically underestimate the scale.

🙋

Jordan is one of the safest countries in the Middle East — extremely welcoming to tourists with very low crime against visitors.

🍽️

Try mansaf (Jordan's national dish — lamb slow-cooked in yoghurt sauce) at any local restaurant. Extraordinary.

💵

US dollars accepted widely. Jordan is more expensive than most expect — budget around £50/day for a comfortable trip.
