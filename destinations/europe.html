// Europe

Europe
Eight thousand years of history, one continent

Europe is the world's most visited continent for a reason — the density of extraordinary things in a small space is unmatched anywhere. Ancient cities, mountain ranges, Mediterranean islands, Arctic wilderness and everything in between.

Europe is simultaneously the easiest and the richest continent to travel. The infrastructure is exceptional, borders are largely open, and the variety within a manageable area is extraordinary — you can be in the Norwegian fjords and on a Greek island within 3 hours of each other by air. The sheer number of extraordinary destinations makes sequencing difficult. This guide links to our in-depth destination guides (more being added regularly), the backpacker budget routes by subregion, and the full ski resort directory for winter trips.

// When to Go

Season guide

Europe's seasons vary dramatically by region. Mediterranean (Italy, Greece, Croatia, Spain, Portugal): May–June and September–October are ideal — warm, uncrowded, affordable. July–August is peak season — hot, crowded and expensive. Northern Europe (Scandinavia, Iceland): June–August for midnight sun; December–February for Northern Lights. Central/Eastern Europe (Prague, Budapest, Balkans): April–May and September–October are perfect; winters are cold but atmospheric. Ski season: December–April across the Alps; late November and May possible at high altitude.

// Need to Know

Before you go

ℹ️

The Schengen Area allows 90 days in 180 across most of Europe on most Western passports — but it's 90 days total, not 90 in each country.

ℹ️

Budget airlines (Ryanair, easyJet, Wizz) make flying between cities extremely cheap — book 6–8 weeks ahead for the best fares.

ℹ️

Train travel in Europe is exceptional — Eurail and Interrail passes are worth it if you're covering multiple countries. Point-to-point tickets booked in advance are often cheaper.

ℹ️

The European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) gives UK citizens access to state healthcare in EU countries. It's free and takes 2 minutes to apply for.

ℹ️

Eastern European countries (Poland, Czech Republic, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria) offer exceptional value — often 40-50% cheaper than Western Europe for identical quality.
