// North America

New York City

The city that invented itself — neighbourhoods, food and relentless forward motion.

💰 £90–£150/day 🗓️ Best: Apr–Jun & Sep–Nov 🛂 ESTA required ($21) 💬 English

The city that never stops

New York is the only city in the world where the mythology matches the reality. The skyline approaching from JFK at night is exactly as extraordinary as every photograph. Central Park in early October is exactly as beautiful. The bagel from a Lower East Side deli at 8am is exactly as good as claimed.

The New York worth visiting is found in the neighbourhoods: the West Village brownstones, the food halls of the Chelsea Market, the Brooklyn waterfront at DUMBO, the Queens food scene along Roosevelt Avenue. The museums are world-class, the bridges are free and the energy is real.

// Quick Facts

💰
Daily Budget
£90–150. NYC is expensive but free museums (many), cheap street food and the subway keep costs manageable.
🚇
Subway
OMNY contactless works on all MTA subway and buses. 7-day unlimited pass (£30) if travelling frequently.
🥯
Bagels
A legitimate NYC bagel is one of the world's great breakfasts — Russ & Daughters, Ess-a-Bagel, or any local deli.
🖼️
Free Museums
The Met (pay-what-you-wish for NY state residents), MoMA is paid. Whitney, Frick, Guggenheim are paid.
🌆
View
The Brooklyn Bridge at dawn and the High Line at sunset give the best city views entirely free.
// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Brooklyn Bridge Walk at Dawn
Walk from Manhattan to Brooklyn at 6am — the view from the centre of the bridge, both skylines visible, completely free and utterly spectacular.
02
The Met Museum — Wing by Wing
One of the world's greatest art museums — don't attempt the whole thing. Egyptian wing, the American wing, the Impressionist galleries. Pay what you wish for NY state residents.
03
High Line at Sunset
The elevated park built on a disused railway line through the Meatpacking District and Chelsea — best in the last hour of light when the city glows.
04
Lower East Side Food Tour
Russ & Daughters smoked salmon, Katz's Deli pastrami, Essex Market dumplings and the immigrant food history of the neighbourhood that built American food culture.
05
DUMBO Brooklyn & the Waterfront
The Manhattan Bridge framing the Brooklyn Bridge from Washington Street — the most photographed view in New York. Walk the Brooklyn Bridge Park waterfront after.
06
Jazz in Harlem or the Village Vanguard
The Village Vanguard in Greenwich Village (every Monday since 1935) or Minton's Playhouse in Harlem — two of the most important jazz venues in the world.
// Areas & Neighbourhoods

Where to base yourself

Manhattan — Downtown & Midtown
Major sights, central
The Brooklyn Bridge, the High Line, Central Park, the museums and the best food halls. Stay in Midtown for convenience or Lower East Side for character.
Brooklyn (Williamsburg/DUMBO/Park Slope)
Local food, waterfront, neighbourhoods
The most interesting dining and neighbourhood culture in New York — Williamsburg for brunch and bars, DUMBO for the skyline view, Park Slope for families.
Queens — Flushing & Astoria
The real food capital, local
Roosevelt Avenue in Jackson Heights is the most diverse food street in the world. Flushing has the best Chinese food outside China.

// When to Go

Season guide

Apr–Jun: The best time — Central Park in bloom, warm enough for the High Line at sunset. Sep–Nov: Excellent — summer heat gone, autumn foliage in Central Park and Prospect Park. Dec–Jan: Magical Christmas energy, ice rinks in the parks. Cold but dry. Jul–Aug: Hot and very crowded. The city is alive but accommodation prices peak.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🚇

Use Oyster-equivalent OMNY contactless on all MTA subway — tap your phone or card. Never buy single-use tickets.

🍕

A dollar slice from a corner pizzeria is a legitimate New York cultural institution — foldable, greasy, enormous. Not dinner, but essential.

🥯

The bagel debate (H&H vs Ess-a-Bagel vs Murray's) is real. Try at least two to form an opinion.

💰

NYC is expensive — accommodation is the main cost. Consider outer boroughs (Williamsburg, Astoria) for significantly cheaper options near the subway.

🚌

The NYC Ferry ($4 flat fare) is the most enjoyable way to travel between Lower Manhattan, Brooklyn and Queens.
