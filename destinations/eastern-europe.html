// Eastern Europe & Balkans

Eastern Europe
& the Balkans

Prague, Budapest, Dubrovnik, Salzburg — the most affordable great cities on the continent.

Eastern Europe offers the best value in Europe — Prague has the most perfectly preserved baroque city on the continent and the cheapest excellent beer anywhere. Budapest has thermal baths, ruin bars and the most dramatic riverside parliament building in Europe. The Dalmatian Coast has medieval walled cities and Adriatic water so blue it looks implausible. And all of it costs 40–50% less than the equivalent experience in Western Europe.

// When to Go

Season guide

Apr–Jun: Best spring weather across the region — warm days, long evenings. Sep–Oct: Excellent autumn, golden light. Dec: Christmas markets are spectacular — Prague and Budapest are among the best in Europe. Jul–Aug: Hot and crowded on the Dalmatian Coast. Ski season: Dec–Apr for Bansko and the Tatras.

// Need to Know

Before you go

💴

Most Eastern European countries use their own currencies — not the Euro. Check before arriving.

✈️

Budget airlines connect most capitals to London for under £50 return with advance booking.

🍺

Always eat and drink in residential neighbourhoods — Old Town tourist pricing is double or triple local rates.

🚂

Overnight trains between capitals (Budapest–Kraków, Prague–Warsaw) are comfortable and scenic.

🏖️

The Dalmatian Coast in July–August is very crowded — May/June and September are dramatically better.
