// Southeast Asia

Philippines — El Nido & Palawan

7,641 islands — the most beautiful waters in Asia and powder-white beaches.

💰 £15–£30/day 🗓️ Best: Nov–May 🛂 No visa (most nationalities, 30 days extendable) 💬 Filipino, English (widely spoken)

7,641 islands, extraordinary water

The Philippines is the most naturally beautiful island nation in Asia — the turquoise limestone lagoons of El Nido, the shipwreck diving of Coron, the surf breaks of Siargao and the 40m visibility reef diving of Tubbataha. The sheer variety of extraordinary beaches and diving sites in a country where English is universally spoken makes it one of Southeast Asia's most accessible destinations.

It requires flying between islands — the Philippines is too spread out for overland travel — but domestic flights are cheap and the reward at each destination is extraordinary.

// Quick Facts

💰
Daily Budget
£15–30. Extremely affordable — guesthouses from £8, excellent food from £2.
✈️
Domestic Flights
Essential — Cebu Pacific and AirAsia Philippines fly between islands cheaply. Book ahead.
🏝️
Island Chain
Luzon (Manila/Palawan), Visayas (Cebu/Boracay) and Mindanao — three major island groups.
🌊
Diving
Coron and Palawan offer some of the best wreck and reef diving in Asia — visibility to 40m.
🌪️
Typhoon Season
Jun–Nov — most pronounced east coast. West coast (Palawan) less affected.
// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
El Nido Island Hopping — Lagoons & Beaches
The limestone karst lagoons of Palawan — Tour A covers the Big Lagoon, Small Lagoon and the most extraordinary snorkelling beaches in Asia.
02
Coron Shipwreck Diving
The Japanese WWII fleet sunk in 1944 — 12 major shipwrecks diveable in crystal-clear water. One of the world's top 10 dive sites.
03
Siargao — Cloud 9 Surf Break
The Philippines' premier surf destination — Cloud 9 is a world-class reef break in a genuinely beautiful island setting.
04
Chocolate Hills of Bohol
1,268 perfectly conical grass-covered hills that turn chocolate brown in the dry season — one of the Philippines' most surreal landscapes.
05
Tubbataha Reef — Liveaboard
The UNESCO marine protected area accessible only March–June — whale sharks, manta rays and the most pristine reef in the Philippines.
06
Intramuros Walking Tour, Manila
The 16th-century Spanish walled city at the heart of Manila — Fort Santiago, the Manila Cathedral and the best context for Philippine colonial history.
// Areas & Neighbourhoods

Where to base yourself

Palawan (El Nido/Coron)
Best islands, limestone lagoons, diving
The most beautiful islands in the Philippines — fly to Puerto Princesa (Coron) or El Nido direct.
Cebu & the Visayas
Diving, whale sharks, waterfalls
The central island group — Cebu City as a hub, Moalboal for diving and Oslob for whale shark swimming.
Siargao
Surf, laid-back island life
The surf island in the northeast — Cloud 9 reef break and the most relaxed island atmosphere in the Philippines.

// When to Go

Season guide

Nov–May: Dry season on the west coast (Palawan) and Visayas — best time. Jun–Oct: Typhoon season most pronounced on the east coast. Palawan less affected. Siargao is best Jun–Nov for surf.

// Need to Know

Before you go

✈️

Domestic flights between islands are essential — Cebu Pacific and AirAsia Philippines are the main carriers. Book 2–4 weeks ahead.

🌊

Philippine reef systems are extraordinary. Bring your own reef-safe sunscreen — many dive operators now require it.

💸

The Philippines is one of the cheapest countries in Southeast Asia — budget travellers can live very well on £20/day.

🌪️

Check typhoon forecasts during Jun–Nov — storms can be severe and cancel ferries.

🤿

Get open-water SCUBA certified before visiting if possible — the diving here is world-class and a certification opens up everything.
