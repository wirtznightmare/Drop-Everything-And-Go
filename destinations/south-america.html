// South America

South
America

Machu Picchu to Patagonia — the continent that rewards everyone who makes the effort to get here.

South America is the continent that earns the longest trips. People who come for two weeks come back for two months. The combination of ancient civilisations, extraordinary wilderness, world-class food (Peru's cuisine is among the best on earth), and genuine affordability in Bolivia, Ecuador and Colombia makes it endlessly compelling. The classic overland route broadly runs Colombia → Ecuador → Peru → Bolivia → Chile/Argentina, or in reverse. Most people fly in and out of Bogotá, Lima or Buenos Aires. Allow at least 6 weeks to do it properly — you'll wish you had more.

// When to Go

Season guide

South America's seasons are the reverse of the Northern Hemisphere. Patagonia is best November–March (Southern Hemisphere summer). Machu Picchu and Peru — dry season is May–October, best for trekking. Bolivia's salt flats are magical year-round but most photogenic in January–March when shallow water creates the mirror effect. Colombia has two dry seasons (Dec–Feb and Jun–Aug) and is largely pleasant year-round. Brazil's Carnival is February — book everything 12 months ahead if you want to experience it.

// Need to Know

Before you go

ℹ️

South America is large — flying between major hubs (Bogotá, Lima, Santiago, Buenos Aires) is often cheaper and much faster than overland.

ℹ️

Altitude sickness is real in Peru and Bolivia — spend 2–3 days acclimatising in Cusco or La Paz before any trekking.

ℹ️

Petty theft is common in major cities (Bogotá, Lima, Rio). Keep electronics out of sight and use hotel safes. Don't flash expensive gear.

ℹ️

The Inca Trail to Machu Picchu requires booking 6 months ahead — permits sell out fast. Alternative routes (Salkantay, Lares) are less restricted.

ℹ️

In Bolivia and Peru, always carry small denomination notes — many places can't break large bills and have no card machines.

ℹ️

Colombia has transformed dramatically — it is now one of the safest and most rewarding countries in South America for travellers.
