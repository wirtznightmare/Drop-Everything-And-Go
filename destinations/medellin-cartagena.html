// South America · Colombia

Medellín & Cartagena

The most transformed city in Latin America and the Caribbean's most beautiful colonial port.

💰 £25–£50/day 🗓️ Best: Dec–Mar & Jul–Aug 🛂 No visa (UK/EU/US, 90 days) 💬 Spanish

Transformation and colonial grandeur

Medellín was the world's most violent city in the 1990s. It is now the most innovative in Latin America — cable cars connect previously inaccessible hillside comunas, the metro system is the best on the continent and the food and nightlife scene rivals any in South America.

Cartagena is the Caribbean counterpoint — the best-preserved Spanish colonial walled city in the Americas. Beautiful and increasingly touristy, but the candy-coloured buildings and 11km walls earn their reputation.

// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Medellín Cable Cars & Commune 13
The MetroCable to the hillside comunas and the open-air street art that tells the city's transformation story — the most remarkable urban renewal narrative in the world.
02
Cartagena Walled City at Dawn
Walk the 11km walls at sunrise before the cruise ships arrive — the candy-coloured buildings of the Centro Histórico largely to yourself.
03
Coffee Region — Salento & Valle de Cocora
The Eje Cafetero — wax palms in the valley, coffee hacienda tours and the colourful town of Salento. 4 hours from Medellín.
04
Ciudad Perdida Trek — 4 Days
The 900-year-old Tairona jungle city — 4 days through rainforest, more authentic than Machu Picchu and far less visited.
05
Medellín Food Scene — El Poblado
The city's restaurant district — the best bandeja paisa, craft beer bars and the late-night fritanga stalls that open at midnight.
06
Tayrona National Park
Jungle-meets-Caribbean — hike through forest to beaches with no road access.

// When to Go

Season guide

Dec–Mar: Dry season on the Caribbean coast — best for Cartagena. Jul–Aug: Second dry season. Apr–Jun & Sep–Nov: Rainy season — travellable but afternoon rain.

// Need to Know

Before you go

✅

Colombia is much safer than its reputation — normal urban awareness in all cities.

🚡

Medellín cable car is included in the regular metro ticket — extraordinary value.

☕

Third-wave coffee shops in Medellín and Bogotá are excellent — the best coffee in the world.

🗺️

Ciudad Perdida must be done with a licensed operator — the trail passes through restricted territory.

🏖️

Cartagena's Getsemaní neighbourhood is more authentic and cheaper than the tourist walled city.
