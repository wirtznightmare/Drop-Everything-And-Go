Medellín & Cartagena
Colombia

The most transformed city in Latin America and the Caribbean's most beautiful colonial port.

South America · Colombia

Why visit Colombia?

Medellín was the world's most violent city in the 1990s. It is now the most innovative in Latin America — cable cars connect previously inaccessible hillside comunas, the metro is the best on the continent and the food and nightlife scene rivals any in South America. This transformation is Colombia's greatest story.

Cartagena is the Caribbean counterpoint — the best-preserved Spanish colonial walled city in the Americas. Beautiful and increasingly touristy, but the candy-coloured buildings and 11km walls earn their reputation completely.

Who It's Perfect For
🚡

Urban Innovation Seekers

The Medellín cable car comunas, the outdoor escalators in San Javier and the urban renewal story are some of the most inspiring examples of city transformation in the world.

☕

Coffee Lovers

The Eje Cafetero coffee region — Salento, wax palms in the Valle de Cocora and coffee hacienda tours — is the most beautiful landscape in Colombia and 4 hours from Medellín.

🏛️

History Travellers

Cartagena's 11km colonial walls, the Castillo San Felipe and the preserved historic centre are the finest examples of Spanish colonial architecture in the Americas.
