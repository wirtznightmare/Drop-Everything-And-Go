// South America · Argentina

Buenos Aires

Tango at midnight, the best steak in the world and a city that runs on pure passion.

💰 £20–£50/day 🗓️ Best: Apr–Jun & Sep–Nov 🛂 No visa (UK/EU/US) 💬 Spanish (Rioplatense)

Tango, parrilla and Malbec

Buenos Aires has the European café culture without the European reserve. The cobblestoned streets of San Telmo, the Parisian avenues of Recoleta, the Sunday feria market and parrillas that stay open until 3am — it is one of the great cities for walking, eating and staying up too late.

The tango clubs (milongas) that run from midnight to dawn are a complete immersion in a living art form. Argentina's economic situation makes it extraordinary value for Western visitors — check the current exchange rate carefully before travelling.

// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
San Telmo Sunday Feria
The cobblestoned antique market every Sunday — silverware, vintage cameras, leather goods and tango dancers performing in the street.
02
Milonga — Community Tango
La Catedral, La Viruta and Club Gricel are the authentic venues. Arrive after midnight, watch for 30 minutes, then join. Accept or decline with the cabeceo nod.
03
Argentine Parrilla Ritual
La Brigada in San Telmo, Don Julio in Palermo — empanadas to start, bife de chorizo carved tableside, Malbec by the bottle. Book ahead.
04
Recoleta Cemetery
Extraordinary ornate mausoleums housing Argentina's most powerful families — Eva Perón's tomb is here. A genuine architectural wonder.
05
Teatro Colón Opera House
One of the world's greatest opera houses — the guided interior tour is extraordinary even without a performance.
06
Palermo Neighbourhood Walk
Tree-lined streets, Sunday organic market, natural wine bars on Thames Street and the best brunch in the city.

// When to Go

Season guide

Apr–Jun: Autumn — the best months for Buenos Aires. Comfortable temperatures, beautiful light. Sep–Nov: Spring, jacaranda in bloom. Dec–Feb: Hot summer. Jul–Aug: Cool winter — great restaurant season, Bariloche ski season.

// Need to Know

Before you go

💱

Research current exchange rates carefully — the informal rate situation changes and the legal implications matter.

🥩

Argentine mealtimes are late — dinner rarely before 9pm. Parrillas peak after 10pm.

💃

For an authentic milonga: arrive after midnight, observe for 30 minutes before joining the floor.

✈️

Fly into Ezeiza International (EZE), not Jorge Newbery (AEP) for international arrivals.

🏧

ATMs have low withdrawal limits — take out the maximum each time.
