// East Asia

East
Asia

Japan, Korea, China, Taiwan — four countries that could each absorb a lifetime.

East Asia is simultaneously the most technologically advanced and most historically layered region on earth. Japan's bullet trains run on 20-second schedules while Kyoto's 1,000-year-old temples sit unchanged in cedar forests. Seoul invented the concept of street food as fine dining. China contains the Great Wall, the Terracotta Warriors, the Li River karsts and 50 UNESCO World Heritage Sites within one country. Taiwan is the region's most underrated destination — extraordinary food, accessible mountains and the most welcoming culture in Asia.

// When to Go

Season guide

Japan: Spring (Mar–May) for cherry blossom; Autumn (Sep–Nov) for foliage. Both are extraordinary and both require advance booking. Winter is ski season. South Korea: Spring and Autumn are ideal — hot and humid in summer. China: Apr–May and Sep–Oct avoid heat and crowds. Avoid Golden Week (first week of May and October). Taiwan: Oct–Mar is comfortable — typhoon season June–September.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🚅

JR Pass for Japan must be bought before you arrive — it cannot be purchased in-country. Worth it if covering 3+ cities.

🔒

Download a VPN before entering China — Google, WhatsApp and Instagram are blocked. ExpressVPN works reliably.

💴

Japan is more affordable than its reputation — £60–80/day covers a lot. Capsule hotels and convenience stores are legitimate strategies.

🗣️

Learn to say thank you in each country's language: Arigatou (Japan), Kamsahamnida (Korea), Xièxiè (China). It goes further than you think.

📱

Google Translate camera mode is invaluable for menus. Download Japanese, Korean and Chinese offline packs before you fly.
