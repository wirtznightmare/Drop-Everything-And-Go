// Africa · South Africa

Cape Town

The flat-topped mountain, African penguins and the world's most beautiful wine estates.

💰 £45–£90/day 🗓️ Best: Oct–Apr 🛂 No visa (UK/EU/US, 90 days) 💬 English, Afrikaans, Xhosa

Mountain, ocean and everything between

Cape Town is one of the most beautiful cities in the world — Table Mountain dominates the city, the Cape Peninsula stretches south and the wine estates of Stellenbosch are 45 minutes away. The combination of urban culture, natural landscape and food is extraordinary.

The city confronts you with its history in unavoidable ways: Robben Island, the District Six Museum and the visible inequality between the city centre and the townships. Cape Town rewards those who engage with its complexity.

// Quick Facts

💰
Daily Budget
£45–90. South African Rand makes it very affordable for European visitors.
🚗
Car Essential
For the Peninsula drive, Boulders Beach penguins and the Winelands.
🌡️
Best Season
Oct–Apr summer — clear skies. May–Sep winter, mild (15°C) and uncrowded Winelands.
🐧
Penguins
Boulders Beach: 3,000 African penguins on a protected beach 45 minutes south.
🍷
Winelands
Stellenbosch and Franschhoek: world-class Pinotage and Chenin Blanc.
// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Table Mountain Cable Car & Summit Walk
The rotating cable car to the flat summit — walk the 3km plateau or hike down through fynbos. The view over the Peninsula.
02
Cape Peninsula Drive — Chapman's Peak
The circular route south — Chapman's Peak Drive, Boulders Beach penguins, Cape Point cliffs and Kalk Bay fishing harbour.
03
Boulders Beach African Penguins
3,000 African penguins on a protected beach 45 minutes south — completely accessible and extraordinary.
04
Robben Island Ferry & Prison Tour
Where Nelson Mandela was imprisoned for 18 years — guided by a former political prisoner.
05
Cape Winelands — Stellenbosch & Franschhoek
The most beautiful wine valley in Africa — Pinotage, Chenin Blanc and Syrah with Huguenot estate architecture.
06
Bo-Kaap & Cape Malay Food
The colourful hillside neighbourhood — bobotie, bredies and koeksisters in the most photogenic streets in Cape Town.

// When to Go

Season guide

Oct–Apr: Summer — best for Table Mountain, beaches and wine estates. May–Sep: Cape winter — mild, empty wine estates, best prices and fireside restaurant season.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🚗

Rent a car — the Peninsula drive, Boulders Beach and Winelands all require one. Good roads throughout.

🛡️

Use Uber not street taxis. Normal urban awareness in the CBD at night.

🍷

Cape wines are extraordinary value in-country. Buy at a farm estate rather than a supermarket.

🐧

Book Boulders Beach in advance during peak summer — limited daily capacity.

⛽

Fill the tank before the Peninsula drive — no petrol stations between Noordhoek and Simon's Town.
