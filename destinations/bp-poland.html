Backpacking Poland

Poland is the best value destination in Europe right now. Warsaw has one of the most extraordinary post-war reconstruction stories in the world — the entire Old Town was rebuilt brick-by-brick from wartime photography after being 90% destroyed. Kraków is perfectly preserved medieval Europe. And the Tatra Mountains offer serious hiking and skiing at a fraction of Alpine prices.

The Highlights

8 Things to Do in Poland

01
Visit Auschwitz-Birkenau — the most important historical site in Europe, requiring a full day
02
Kraków Old Town and Wawel Castle — the UNESCO medieval centre is perfectly preserved
03
Warsaw Rising Museum — the most powerful World War II museum in the world
04
Wieliczka Salt Mine near Kraków — underground chapels carved entirely from salt
05
Białowieża Forest — the last primeval forest in Europe, home to European bison
06
Gdańsk old town and the shipyards — where Solidarity was born and the Cold War began to end
07
Tatra Mountains at Zakopane — hiking and skiing at a fraction of Alpine prices
08
Eat pierogi, żurek (sour rye soup) and bigos at a milk bar (Bar Mleczny) — authentic and very cheap

Insider Knowledge

What Most Guides Don't Tell You About Poland

  • →

    Poland uses the Złoty (PLN) — not the Euro. Exchange at banks or ATMs, never at exchange kiosks

  • →

    Always eat at a Bar Mleczny (milk bar) — traditional Polish food at £2–4 for a full meal, subsidised and authentic

  • →

    Bolt taxi app works everywhere in Poland — significantly cheaper and more reliable than hailing taxis

  • →

    Book Auschwitz tickets well in advance — the guided tours sell out completely and independent entry is limited

  • →

    The PKP Intercity night train between Warsaw and Kraków is excellent — arrive early morning, sleeper very cheap
