// South America · Bolivia

Uyuni Salt Flats

10,000 square kilometres of white salt reflecting an infinite sky.

💰 £15–£30/day 🗓️ Best: Jan–Mar (mirror) & May–Oct (dry) 🛂 No visa for UK nationals 💬 Spanish, Quechua, Aymara

Sky above, sky below

The Salar de Uyuni is the world's largest salt flat — during the rainy season a thin layer of water creates the world's largest natural mirror, reflecting the sky in both directions until there is no horizon. It is one of the most extraordinary photography opportunities in nature.

Bolivia is the cheapest country in South America and also contains the Death Road (now a mountain bike route), Lake Titicaca and La Paz at 3,600m. It demands patience and acclimatisation but rewards both.

// Quick Facts

💰
Daily Budget
£15–30. The cheapest country in South America by a significant margin.
🌧️
Mirror Effect
Rainy season Jan–Mar. Dry season May–Oct is easier to travel but the flat is white not mirrored.
🏔️
Altitude
La Paz at 3,600m. Salt flat approach at 4,700m. Arrive from lower altitude.
🚲
Death Road
64km mountain bike descent — extremely well-organised by operators now.
🛻
Tours
3-day 4WD tours from Uyuni town (£20–40) are the standard approach.
// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Salt Flat Mirror Effect — Jan to Mar
The shallow rainy season water transforms the flat into a perfect sky mirror — the most extraordinary natural photography in South America.
02
Death Road Mountain Bike Descent
The 64km descent from 4,700m to 1,200m through cloud forest on the former World's Most Dangerous Road — now entirely safe with good operators.
03
Cactus Island at Sunset
Isla Incahuasi — giant cacti in the middle of the flat at sunset when the salt turns pink and the horizon disappears.
04
La Paz Cable Cars & Witches Market
The world's highest administrative capital — the Mercado de las Brujas, cholita wrestling and the extraordinary cable car system above the city.
05
Lake Titicaca & Isla del Sol
The world's highest navigable lake — the sacred Incan island where creation mythology says civilisation began.
06
Potosí Silver Mine Tour
The mine that funded the Spanish colonial empire — still working 400 years later. Tours with miners are profound.

// When to Go

Season guide

Jan–Mar: Mirror effect — rainy season makes roads harder but photography extraordinary. May–Oct: Dry season — easier travel, white flat (not mirrored). Nov–Dec & Apr: Shoulder — sometimes mirror effect possible.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🏔️

Altitude sickness at La Paz (3,600m) and the flat approach (4,700m). Arrive from Cusco or Arequipa rather than flying from sea level.

🍵

Coca leaves and coca tea are traditional altitude remedies — legal in Bolivia and genuinely helpful.

💰

Bolivia is the cheapest country in South America — £15/day is realistic.

🚲

Choose a Death Road operator who provides full-suspension bikes, good helmets and an experienced guide.

🌧️

Rainy season (Jan–Mar) tours require high-clearance 4WD. In dry season any 4WD works.
