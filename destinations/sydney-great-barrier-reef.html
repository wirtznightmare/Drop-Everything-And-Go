// Oceania · Australia

Sydney & the Great Barrier Reef

The most photogenic harbour in the world and the largest living structure visible from space.

💰 £60–£100/day 🗓️ Best: Sep–Nov & Mar–May 🛂 eVisitor or ETA required (apply online) 💬 English

Opera House, reef and the outback

Sydney is the most beautifully situated city in Australia — the Opera House and Harbour Bridge framing the largest natural harbour in the world, Bondi Beach 20 minutes from the CBD. The Great Barrier Reef off Queensland is 2,300km of coral, the world's largest living structure.

Together they represent the most compelling Australian itinerary for first-time visitors — Sydney for the urban experience, Cairns for the reef and, if time allows, Uluru for the spiritual heart of the country.

// Top Experiences

What to actually do

01
Sydney Harbour at Dawn — Opera House View
Mrs Macquarie's Chair at sunrise for the Opera House and Harbour Bridge together — the most photographed view in Australia, free and at its best before 7am.
02
Bondi to Coogee Coastal Walk
The 6km cliff path — Bondi Icebergs pool, Tamarama, Bronte and Coogee. One of the world's great urban coastal walks.
03
Great Barrier Reef Snorkelling from Cairns
Day trip to the Outer Reef or multi-day liveaboard — marine biologist guides on most boats.
04
Daintree Rainforest — World's Oldest
The 180-million-year-old tropical forest north of Cairns — cassowaries, crocs and the reef visible from the beach where jungle meets sea.
05
Blue Mountains Day Trip from Sydney
Sandstone escarpments 90 minutes west — Katoomba, the Three Sisters and the world's steepest railway at Scenic World.
06
Uluru at Sunrise
The sacred sandstone monolith — sunrise and sunset colour changes are extraordinary. The Tjukurpa cultural centre provides essential context.

// When to Go

Season guide

Sep–Nov: Best — dry, warm, uncrowded. Mar–May: Excellent autumn. Jun–Aug: Best for the Reef (no jellyfish). Dec–Feb: Reef jellyfish season, Sydney is hot and busy.

// Need to Know

Before you go

🛂

eVisitor (free) or ETA ($20) must be applied online — not available at the border.

🦺

Working Holiday Visa (subclass 417) for under-30s — apply at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au.

☀️

Australian UV is extreme — SPF50+ even on cloudy days.

🚗

Driving between cities is required — distances are enormous. Internal flights are cheap.

🐊

Crocodiles in tropical north are real and dangerous. Obey all warning signs near water.
