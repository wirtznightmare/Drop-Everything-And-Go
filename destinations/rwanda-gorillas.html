Rwanda — Gorilla Trekking
Rwanda

One hour with mountain gorillas — the most intimate wildlife experience on earth.

Africa · Rwanda

Why visit Rwanda?

One hour with a habituated mountain gorilla family in the Virunga volcanoes is the most intimate wildlife experience available anywhere. The gorillas are within metres. There are fewer than 1,100 mountain gorillas left in the world — all of them in the Virunga mountains shared between Rwanda, Uganda and DRC.

Rwanda has also undergone one of the most extraordinary national transformations in modern history — from the 1994 genocide to one of the most stable, cleanest and fastest-developing countries in Africa in 30 years. Kigali is now the cleanest and most organised capital in sub-Saharan Africa.

The Highlights

Things to Do & See

01
Mountain gorilla trek in Volcanoes National Park — one hour with a habituated family in the Virunga forest
02
Kigali Genocide Memorial — the memorial to 800,000 killed in 100 days, profound and essential
03
Golden monkey trek — the endemic Virunga golden monkeys for $100, far less visited than gorillas
04
Nyungwe Forest chimpanzee trek and the 200m suspended canopy walkway
05
Lake Kivu — the volcanically-heated lake on the Congo border for kayaking and the best sunset in Rwanda
06
Kigali city tour — the extraordinary transformation story, the urban planning and the Kimironko market
07
Volcanoes National Park camping — overnight in the park for dawn gorilla tracking
08
Musanze Caves — the underground lava tubes used by locals during the genocide and pre-colonial period
