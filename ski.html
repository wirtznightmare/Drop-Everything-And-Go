Ski guides: from my own trips

I've skied every resort on this page myself, whether that was with family, with friends, on a uni trip or on a school trip.

I've taken the old list of resorts down, so this page only has places I've actually skied. Each guide covers where I stayed, the runs I loved, the mistakes I made and how to get there (including by train where that works).

If I can't remember something, like the name of a restaurant from a trip years ago, I'll say so rather than make it up.

Lower carbon

Getting there by train

I've done most of these trips by flying to Geneva and getting a transfer. A lot of resorts are easier to reach by train than you'd think, though, and it cuts the carbon of a ski trip by a lot. Here's the nearest station for each one.

Les GetsCluses, then about 30 minutes by bus
ChampéryChampéry, by mountain railway from Aigle
ChamonixChamonix, via Saint-Gervais and the Mont Blanc Express
La ThuileAosta, then a bus (the slowest of the lot)
Val ThorensMoûtiers, then about an hour by bus
Les Deux AlpesGrenoble, then a bus
ObergurglÖtztal, then just over an hour by bus
YlläsKolari, on the overnight sleeper from Helsinki
