// Pirin Mountains

Why ski Bansko?

Bansko has spent two decades developing into one of the most credible budget ski destinations in Europe. The UNESCO-protected old town is genuinely beautiful with cobbled streets and traditional mehana taverns. The skiing covers 75km and is best for beginners and intermediates, with lift pass prices at roughly a third of the equivalent in France or Switzerland. A seven-day ski holiday in Bansko costs less than a long weekend in Val d'Isère.

// Insider Knowledge

Ski the mountain in the morning when the gondola queues are short, then spend your afternoon in the old town drinking rakia and eating banitsa. The skiing doesn't take long to exhaust but the culture is genuinely interesting.
