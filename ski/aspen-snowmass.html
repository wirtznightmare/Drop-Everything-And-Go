// Colorado

Why ski Aspen?

Aspen operates on four distinct mountains — Aspen Mountain (known as Ajax), Aspen Highlands, Buttermilk and Snowmass — all covered by one lift pass. Each has a distinct personality: Ajax is steep and expert-heavy, Highlands has the legendary Highland Bowl, Buttermilk is perfect for beginners and hosts the X Games, and Snowmass is simply enormous (3,362 acres). The town of Aspen is where celebrities holiday and the food, hotels and culture reflect that.

// Insider Knowledge

Fly into Denver and drive (4 hours) rather than paying the premium fares into tiny Aspen Airport. The I-70 through the Rockies is spectacular and a rental car gives you flexibility for day trips to Vail or Breckenridge.
