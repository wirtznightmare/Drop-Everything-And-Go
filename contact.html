// FAQ

Common Questions

How much does trip planning cost? +

Bespoke itinerary planning starts from £150 for a personalised day-by-day plan with accommodation recommendations and transport advice. Full booking management (where we research, compare and book everything) is priced per trip based on complexity.

How long does it take? +

An initial itinerary draft is typically returned within 24–48 hours. Most trips move from first contact to fully planned in 1–2 weeks. For complex multi-destination trips or peak-season travel, 4–8 weeks of lead time is ideal.

Do you plan all budgets? +

Yes — from budget backpacking (£15–30/day) to luxury honeymoons and group trips. The goal is always the same: the best possible experience for your specific budget, not an upsell.

Do you offer travel insurance? +

We don't sell insurance directly but we always recommend suitable providers for each trip type and can advise on what level of cover makes sense for your itinerary.
