FAQ

Common Questions

Can you plan my trip? +

On a small scale, yes. I can put together a suggested plan with places to stay, how to get around and what's worth doing, and you book everything yourself directly with the airline, hotel or operator. That way you keep their booking protections. I don't sell or book holidays.

Can I suggest a place to write about? +

Please do. I only write trip reports about places I've been, so suggestions go on the list for future trips.

Do you work with brands? +
