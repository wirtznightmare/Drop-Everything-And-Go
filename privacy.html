Privacy & Disclosure

Last updated: March 2026

1. Who We Are

Drop Everything And Go is a travel blog and bespoke trip planning service based in the United Kingdom, run by Blake Wirtz. This policy covers how we handle your data and discloses our affiliate relationships.

Questions? Email [email protected].

2. Affiliate Disclosure

This site currently uses one affiliate partner: GetYourGuide.

When you click a GetYourGuide link on this site and book a tour or activity, we earn a small commission. This costs you nothing — the price you pay is identical to booking direct.

We only link to GetYourGuide experiences we'd genuinely recommend. Affiliate income does not influence what gets recommended — if something isn't worth booking, we don't link to it.

This disclosure is made in line with UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) guidelines and the UK Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008.

If we add further affiliate partnerships in future, they will be disclosed here and on relevant pages of the site. We will never have undisclosed commercial relationships.

3. Information We Collect

What you give us directly:

  • Name and email when you subscribe to our newsletter
  • Name, email and trip details when you submit a planning enquiry

What's collected automatically when you visit:

  • IP address, browser type, pages viewed (via standard analytics)
  • Clicks on GetYourGuide affiliate links — recorded anonymously by GetYourGuide's tracking system

We use Formspree to process contact form submissions. When you submit a form, that data is stored by Formspree and forwarded to us.

4. How We Use It

  • To reply to your trip planning enquiries and deliver our service
  • To send our newsletter — only if you subscribed, unsubscribe any time
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties for marketing. Full stop.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies (needed for the site to function) and analytics cookies (to understand page views, anonymised). If you click a GetYourGuide link, GetYourGuide may set their own tracking cookies to attribute any booking commission — this is standard affiliate tracking and does not store your personal details.

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies via the banner on your first visit.

6. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold. To exercise any of these rights, email [email protected] and we'll respond within 30 days.

7. Changes

This policy will be updated if our affiliate relationships change — particularly when new partners are added. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.

Plain English summary: The only affiliate link on this site right now is GetYourGuide. If you book through one of those links, we get a small commission and you pay the same price. Your data is never sold. That's it.
