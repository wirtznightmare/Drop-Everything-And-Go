Privacy & Disclosure

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who we are

Drop Everything And Go is a travel blog based in the United Kingdom, run by Blake Adams. This page explains what data the site collects, which cookies it uses, how the site makes money and the terms for using it.

Questions? Email dropeverythingandgo2026@gmail.com.

2. Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you book through one, I earn a small commission and you pay the same price.

Current partners:

  • GetYourGuide: tours, tickets and activities.
  • Travelpayouts: an affiliate network used on some blog posts. Its script can turn ordinary links to travel sites (for example hotels or flights) into affiliate links. It only runs if you accept cookies.

Affiliate links are marked on each page that uses them. Commission doesn't decide what I recommend: if something wasn't worth it, I don't link to it. Nobody pays to be featured, and there are no sponsored reviews.

This disclosure follows the UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) and CMA guidance on affiliate marketing. If I add partners, they'll be listed here and the date at the top will change.

You can also support the site through Buy Me a Coffee. That's a voluntary tip and doesn't affect any content.

3. Information collected

What you give directly:

  • Your email address when you subscribe to the newsletter
  • Your name, email and trip details when you send a planning enquiry

Both forms are handled by Formspree, which stores the submission and forwards it to me by email.

What's collected automatically, only if you accept cookies:

  • Pages viewed, device and browser type, and approximate location, via Google Analytics (IP anonymisation on)
  • Affiliate clicks and bookings, via GetYourGuide and Travelpayouts tracking

The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and the Buy Me a Coffee button from its own servers. Like any website request, these see your IP address.

4. How it's used

  • To reply to your enquiry and plan your trip if you go ahead
  • To send the newsletter, only if you subscribed (unsubscribe any time)
  • To see which pages are useful and improve the site

Your data is never sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

5. Cookies

No non-essential cookies are set until you press Accept on the cookie banner. If you press Decline, analytics and affiliate scripts don't load at all.

  • Essential: one small setting in your browser's local storage (ck) that remembers your choice.
  • Analytics (if accepted): Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_*), kept for up to 2 years.
  • Affiliate (if accepted): Travelpayouts and GetYourGuide cookies that record a click so a booking can be credited. GetYourGuide may also set its own cookies once you're on its website.

Change your cookie choice

6. Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data I hold about you. Email dropeverythingandgo2026@gmail.com and I'll respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

7. Terms of use

  • Information only. Guides are written in good faith from my own trips and research. Prices, opening times, transport and rules change, so check with the operator before you book or travel.
  • Your own judgement. Skiing, hiking and off-piste travel carry real risks. Follow local advice, use qualified guides where recommended, and get proper travel insurance.
  • Trip planning help. If I help plan a trip for you, I give recommendations only. You make bookings directly with airlines, hotels and operators, and their terms apply to those bookings. I'm not a travel agent or tour operator and don't sell package holidays.
  • Third-party sites. I'm not responsible for the content or bookings on sites I link to.
  • Copyright. Words and photos on this site are mine unless credited. Please ask before reusing them.

8. Changes

This page is updated when partners, tools or terms change. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Plain English summary: Nothing tracks you unless you accept cookies. Some links earn me a small commission (GetYourGuide and Travelpayouts), at no extra cost to you. Your data is never sold.
